Print

Each slot decides the print size of its photo. Save prints gives you one zip with a folder per size: the original XL and L photos, and the S photos combined three at a time into 18×8 cm strips (one 6×8 cm photo each, with a thin white line to cut along). The album plan PDF shows where every photo and note goes.
